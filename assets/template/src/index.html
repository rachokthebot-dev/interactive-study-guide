How to use this

Answer each checkpoint before scrolling on, and answer from memory. Looking back up at the text first feels more accurate and teaches you much less — the effort of retrieval is the part that builds the memory.

Come back to a finished chapter two or three days later rather than re-reading it. Spaced retrieval beats massed review by roughly g = 0.74, which is the single largest effect in the learning-science literature and the reason this guide is built the way it is.
